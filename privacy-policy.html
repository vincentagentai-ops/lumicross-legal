Privacy Policy

“Lumicross” app — last updated: October 29, 2026

In one sentence: Lumicross is a free logic puzzle game with no account, published by Vincent's Tech Studio, that collects no personal data itself: your progress stays on your device. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, and the optional leaderboards and achievements by Google Play Games Services.

1. Who provides this app

Lumicross is a logic puzzle game for Android published by Vincent's Tech Studio (“we”). This policy explains what data the app processes. Questions: vincent.agent.ai@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, streak, glows, palettes, achievements, settings, puzzle in progress) and anonymous usage counters (for example, how many levels were started) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up this progress to your Google account; this is handled by Google and Android, not by us. Uninstalling the app deletes this data; “Settings › Reset progress” erases it too.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads (an interstitial between journey levels and optional rewarded ads) through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device and the app, and your interactions with ads;
  • diagnostic data (crash and performance information of the SDK).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent with Google’s User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent management platform) before any ad request. If you do not consent, only limited or non-personalized ads may be shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options. Ads are limited to content rated PG. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your scores (daily best time, levels completed, total stars, best streak) and unlocked achievements are sent to Google Play Games, linked to your Play Games profile. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can play without signing in.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not run our own servers and do not receive your data.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • The app requests no sensitive permission: only internet and network state. The ads SDK adds the advertising ID permission and technical permissions that give no access to your information (Android’s privacy-preserving ads APIs, keeping the device awake during a video, foreground service).
  • There are no in-app purchases.

6. Children

Lumicross is intended for adults (target audience 18+) and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Data security and retention

Data exchanged with Google services is encrypted in transit. Google retains advertising and Play Games data according to its own policies. Data stored on your device stays there until you reset it or uninstall the app.

8. Your rights

You can withdraw advertising consent in Settings › Privacy options, reset your advertising ID in your Android settings, and manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com. Because we hold no data about you, we cannot access, correct or delete anything on our side; the data on your device can be erased with “Reset progress” or by uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the app’s data practices change, and change the date at the top.
